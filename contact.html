What we can help with

  • A wrong rate, threshold or calculation result
  • A calculator or layout that does not work
  • Accessibility problems with a keyboard or screen reader
  • Privacy requests about this site
  • Suggestions for a guide or worked example

What we cannot do

We cannot give personal tax advice, handle an HMRC case, check your VAT return or act as your accountant. For those, use a qualified adviser or contact HMRC.
