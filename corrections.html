Corrections log

Last updated 17 November 2026

A public record of corrections that changed a figure, a rule or the way a page works. Style edits are not listed; anything that could have misled a reader is.

Report a correction

Send the page, the sentence and the source you are relying on through the contact page, or open an issue in the public issue tracker. A link to GOV.UK, an HMRC notice or legislation.gov.uk is the fastest route to a fix.

What counts as a correction

A correction is a change to a fact that was wrong, incomplete in a way that changed the answer, or stated with more certainty than the source supports. Corrections are made on the page, the review date is updated where a fact changed, and material corrections are listed below.

We do not quietly delete an incorrect page. If content is merged or retired, the change is recorded and the replacement is linked.

What is not a correction

  • A rate or threshold changed by HMRC or a Budget. That is an update, and it is applied with a new review date.
  • A different but defensible reading of guidance where HMRC has not settled the point.
  • Rewording, layout, navigation or accessibility changes that leave the meaning intact.

Questions about this policy can be raised on the contact page or in the public issue tracker.
