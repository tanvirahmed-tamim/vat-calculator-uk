UK VAT COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
VAT in UK
https://vatinuk.co.uk
Checked against GOV.UK and HMRC guidance, November 2026

This checklist is a practical prompt sheet, not tax or legal advice. Check GOV.UK for the rules that apply to your business.

REGISTRATION
[ ] Add up taxable turnover for the last 12 months at the end of every month.
[ ] Register with HMRC if the total exceeds GBP 90,000, or if you expect it to exceed GBP 90,000 in the next 30 days.
[ ] Tell HMRC within 30 days of the end of the month in which you crossed the threshold.
[ ] Record your VAT registration date and VAT number.
[ ] Consider voluntary registration if you buy a lot from VAT-registered suppliers.
[ ] Check the GBP 88,000 deregistration threshold: you can ask to cancel if you expect taxable turnover in the next 12 months to stay below it.

INVOICES
[ ] Show your business name, address and VAT number.
[ ] Use a unique invoice number and show the tax point.
[ ] Show the customer's name and address.
[ ] Describe the goods or services supplied.
[ ] Show the net total, the VAT rate and the VAT amount for each rate.
[ ] Show the gross total.
[ ] Use a simplified invoice only for retail supplies up to GBP 250 including VAT.
[ ] Keep copies of invoices and credit notes issued.

RECORDS AND FILING
[ ] Keep digital records and file through Making Tax Digital software.
[ ] Reconcile sales and purchases to the boxes on the VAT return.
[ ] Check box 1 output tax, box 4 input tax and box 5 the difference.
[ ] Include standard, reduced, zero-rated and exempt sales in box 6; there is no separate exempt box.
[ ] File and pay by one calendar month and seven days after the end of the period.
[ ] Keep VAT records for six years.

INPUT TAX
[ ] Confirm each cost is genuinely for business use.
[ ] Hold a valid VAT invoice or receipt for every claim.
[ ] Check for blocked costs: business entertainment of UK clients, most cars available for private use, and private spending.
[ ] Record the business-use percentage for mixed-use costs.
[ ] Claim input tax within four years of the return period it relates to.
