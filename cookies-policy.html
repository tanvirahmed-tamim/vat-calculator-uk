Cookies policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This site does not set cookies, which is why you do not see a cookie consent banner. This statement was last verified on 13 November 2026.

Summary

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels or tracking identifiers are used.
  • No cookie consent banner is needed, because there is nothing optional to consent to.
  • Share links keep the calculation in the URL fragment on your device.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. Cookies are often used to keep you signed in, remember preferences or measure how a site is used. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage and tracking pixels.

This site does not use any of them. There is no account to keep you signed in to and no preference that needs to persist between visits.

Storage on this site

The table below lists the storage this site actually uses. The only entry is the URL fragment, which is part of the address rather than a cookie.

Storage used by VAT in UK
Type Name Purpose Duration
Cookies None Not used —
Local or session storage None Not used —
URL fragment #amount=… Keeps a calculation in a shareable link Until you change or close the page

The URL fragment is processed only by your browser. It is not sent to a server when you load the page, and it is not used to identify you.

Analytics and advertising

There is no Google Analytics, no advertising network, no social media pixel, no heatmap script and no A/B testing tool. The page loads one local stylesheet and one local script, and nothing from a third-party domain.

Third-party links

Pages link to GOV.UK and other external sites. Those sites may set their own cookies once you follow a link. Their cookie and privacy policies apply from that point, not this one.

If this changes

If analytics or any other cookie-based feature is ever added, this policy will be updated first and appropriate consent controls will be introduced before anything is set. The review date at the top of this page will change at the same time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page. General data rights are explained in the privacy policy.
