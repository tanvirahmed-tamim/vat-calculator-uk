About VAT in UK

Last reviewed 10 November 2026

An independent, free VAT calculator for the United Kingdom, built to answer one question quickly and show its working.

What this site does

VAT in UK adds VAT to a net price, removes VAT from a gross price, and shows the net, VAT and gross amounts side by side. It supports the standard 20% rate, the reduced 5% rate, the zero rate and any custom rate you enter.

Around the main calculator are practical tools: a bulk calculator for invoice lines, a registration threshold check, a Flat Rate Scheme estimate, a rate history table and four specialist calculators for fractions, the margin scheme, the domestic reverse charge and VAT on expenses.

There is no account, no sign-up and no paywall. Results update as you type, and the calculator keeps working if your connection drops after the page has loaded.

Who it is for

The site is written for sole traders, freelancers, small limited companies, bookkeepers, landlords, charities and anyone who needs to check a VAT figure without opening a spreadsheet. It is also used by students and by people checking a quote or invoice before they pay it.

It is a calculator and reference site, not an accounting package. It does not file returns, store records or connect to HMRC.

How the calculations work

Adding VAT multiplies the net amount by one plus the rate as a decimal:

  • Gross = Net × (1 + rate ÷ 100)
  • At 20%: £100 × 1.20 = £120
  • At 5%: £100 × 1.05 = £105
  • At 0%: the gross equals the net

Removing VAT divides the gross amount by the same factor:

  • Net = Gross ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)
  • At 20%: £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100, with £20 of VAT
  • At 5%: £105 ÷ 1.05 = £100, with £5 of VAT

All money is handled in pence and rounded to the nearest penny at the point where VAT is calculated. The same rule is used across the calculator, the bulk tool and the specialist pages.

The adding VAT guide and the removing VAT guide explain the reasoning with more examples.

Editorial standards and accuracy

Every figure published by VAT in UK is checked against two primary sources: the Value Added Tax Act 1994, including Schedule 1 for registration thresholds, and the current HMRC VAT Notice 700 (the VAT guide) or a more specific notice where one applies. HMRC VAT Notice 700/1 is the reference for registration and deregistration questions.

Content is reviewed on a scheduled cycle and whenever a rate, threshold or deadline changes. Each page shows the date of its last review, and that date moves only when the underlying facts have been rechecked, not when a page is reformatted.

  • Rates and liability: GOV.UK VAT rates and HMRC VAT Notice 700.
  • Registration: Value Added Tax Act 1994 Schedule 1 and HMRC VAT Notice 700/1.
  • Calculation: net × (1 + rate ÷ 100), with VAT rounded to the nearest penny.
  • Cross-border trade: HMRC place of supply guidance and the Windsor Framework rules.

If you spot an error, please tell us through the contact page. Corrections are reviewed and, where confirmed, published with an updated review date. We do not publish sponsored content, guest posts or paid links.

The GOV.UK pages checked most often are VAT rates, when to register for VAT, the Flat Rate Scheme and VAT invoices. Every page also lists the specific sources behind its own figures.

Independence and funding

VAT in UK is independent of HMRC and of any accountancy firm. It is not a government service and it does not give tax advice. The site carries no advertising, no affiliate links and no third-party analytics, and it is free to use.

Accessibility

Pages use semantic headings, landmarks and form labels. Interactive controls are reachable by keyboard, focus states are visible, and the layout works at 200% zoom and at narrow mobile widths. Motion is reduced automatically when your device asks for it.

If something is difficult to use with a screen reader, keyboard or magnification, please report it through the contact page so it can be fixed.

Privacy architecture

VAT in UK is a static HTML, CSS and JavaScript application. Every sum is computed in the browser. No amount, invoice line or bulk file is transmitted to a server, because the site has no server-side calculation endpoint and no database of user figures.

The site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, fonts, pixels or analytics, and requests no external assets after the page has loaded. The only network traffic is the page and its own files. The full detail is in the privacy policy and the cookies policy.

Contact and corrections

For corrections, feedback or accessibility reports, use the contact page. We aim to reply to genuine enquiries within a few working days. Please do not send confidential tax records or personal data.

Not tax advice. The content on this site is general information. VAT treatment depends on your circumstances and the rules in force at the tax point. Confirm with GOV.UK or a qualified adviser before you file.
