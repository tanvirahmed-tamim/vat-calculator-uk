Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use VAT in UK, in plain English.

Who we are

VAT in UK is the data controller for this website. The site is run by the VAT in UK team, an independent publisher with no affiliation to HMRC. Because we do not operate a public email address, privacy requests are handled through the contact page.

The short version

  • Every calculation runs locally in your browser.
  • We do not have user accounts and we do not ask you to sign in.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics or profiling.
  • We do not set cookies.
  • Contact messages are prepared in your browser and are not transmitted by this site.

What we process

Calculator inputs

Amounts, rates, modes and tool inputs are processed in your browser only. They are not sent to us, logged by us or stored in a database.

Share links

The Share button places the current calculation in the part of the web address after the # symbol. That fragment stays on your device and in your browser history. It is not sent to a server, and it is not a cookie.

Contact form

The contact form builds a message locally, then copies it or downloads it as a text file. Nothing is transmitted to us by the form. If you later send that message through another channel, that channel's privacy terms apply.

Hosting logs

If you reach the site through a web host, that provider may process standard technical logs such as your IP address, browser type, requested page and time of request, for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify individual visitors.

Lawful basis

Where hosting logs are processed, the lawful basis is our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure and available. If you choose to contact us, we process what you send on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding. Where the law requires records to be kept, the basis is legal obligation.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies, so there is no cookie banner. The full detail, including what would change if analytics were ever added, is in the cookies policy. This statement was last verified on 13 November 2026.

How long we keep information

We do not retain calculator inputs or share links. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any follow-up, and is then deleted. Hosting logs are kept by the provider for its own short retention period.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data or share it for marketing. A hosting provider may process technical logs in another country under its own safeguards. We do not transfer calculator data anywhere because it never leaves your browser.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to erase data in certain circumstances;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • request portability of data you provided;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, use the contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Security

The site is static and has no login, database or payment flow, which removes most common data risks. Any message you choose to send should be limited to what is needed for your enquiry.

Children

The site is intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts to process personal data in a new way, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent through the contact page. Please do not include confidential tax records, bank details or identification documents.
