VAT on Business Entertainment: What You Can Reclaim

By the VAT in UK team · Last reviewed 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Part of Reclaiming VAT

Business entertainment is one of the few costs where the VAT block is deliberate. Entertaining a UK client is usually a private benefit, so the input tax is not recoverable even when the expense is allowed for income tax.

In short

VAT on entertaining UK clients is blocked. VAT on staff entertainment is generally recoverable when it is for business purposes, and entertainment of overseas customers can be recovered subject to conditions. Business gifts over £50 may create output tax.

Who this is for: Businesses that entertain clients, provide staff events, take customers to meals or give business gifts.

The block

VAT on business entertainment is blocked for anyone other than an employee or an overseas customer. "Entertainment" includes hospitality of any kind: meals, drinks, theatre tickets, sporting events, trips and similar hospitality.

The block applies even when the expense is wholly for business purposes and even when it is allowed for income tax. The VAT is a real cost to the business, not an input tax it can reclaim.

The block does not apply to the cost of transporting or accommodating a customer if that cost is not itself entertainment. A train fare to a business meeting is travel, not entertainment; a meal during the trip is entertainment.

Staff entertainment

VAT on staff entertainment is generally recoverable, because employees are excluded from the block. A Christmas party, a team meal or a staff away day can therefore generate recoverable input tax when it is for business purposes.

If employees can bring guests, the guest element is still staff entertainment for VAT, but the income tax treatment may differ. Keep the invitation, the purpose and the attendee list with the invoice.

Overseas customers

Entertainment of an overseas customer is not blocked, provided the customer is not resident in the UK and the entertainment is for business purposes. The exception is specific, and the evidence should show who the customer is and where they belong.

If a UK-based employee of an overseas company attends, the position can be different. Look at the customer's residence and the nature of the hospitality, not just the company's name.

Subsistence

VAT on an employee's own food and drink while travelling for business is generally recoverable as subsistence, subject to the normal rules. It is not entertainment of a client.

Keep the receipt and record the business journey. A meal with a client is entertainment and is blocked; a meal eaten alone during a business trip is subsistence and may be recoverable.

Business gifts

If you give a business gift costing more than £50 excluding VAT to the same person in a 12-month period, you may have to account for output tax on the gift. There are exceptions for samples and for gifts that are not part of a series.

The £50 limit is per recipient per year, not per gift. Keep a record of gifts so the total can be checked. The reclaiming VAT on expenses guide covers the wider recovery rules.

Common mistakes

  • Reclaiming VAT on a client meal because the expense is allowed for income tax.
  • Treating a client's travel as entertainment when it is a separate travel cost.
  • Assuming all overseas entertainment is recoverable without evidence.
  • Ignoring the £50 limit on business gifts.

Questions

Can I reclaim VAT on entertaining clients?
No, VAT on entertaining UK clients is blocked. It is a real cost to the business, even when the expense is allowed for income tax.
Can I reclaim VAT on a staff Christmas party?
Yes, generally. Staff entertainment is recoverable when it is for business purposes, because employees are excluded from the block.
Can I reclaim VAT on entertaining overseas customers?
Yes, subject to conditions. The customer must not be resident in the UK and the entertainment must be for business purposes.
Is a meal while travelling for business recoverable?
Usually yes, as subsistence. A meal with a client is entertainment and is blocked; a meal eaten alone on a business trip is different.
Do I pay VAT on business gifts?
If you give gifts costing more than £50 excluding VAT to the same person in a 12-month period, you may have to account for output tax, unless an exception applies.
Is a client's train fare entertainment?
No, usually not. Travel to a business meeting is a travel cost, not entertainment. A meal during the trip is entertainment.
