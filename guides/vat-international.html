International and cross-border VAT

Maintained by the VAT in UK team · Last reviewed 17 November 2026 · 9 min read

Cross-border VAT turns on one question: where is the supply made? Once you know the place of supply, the rate, the registration and the evidence usually follow.

In short

Exports of goods are usually zero-rated, imports can attract import VAT, and B2B services to overseas clients are often outside the scope of UK VAT. The place of supply decides which country taxes the sale.

Start with place of supply

Place of supply is the rule that decides which country has the right to tax a sale. For goods it usually follows where the goods are when sold or delivered. For services it usually follows the customer for B2B supplies and the supplier for B2C supplies, with special rules for digital services, land and transport.

Once the place of supply is outside the UK, you normally do not charge UK VAT. That is not the same as zero-rating: an export of goods is zero-rated, while a B2B service to an overseas customer is usually outside the scope.

Goods

Exports are zero-rated when you keep evidence that the goods left the UK. Imports attract import VAT, and customs duty may apply separately. Postponed VAT accounting lets VAT-registered importers declare import VAT on their VAT return instead of paying it at the border.

Services

Most B2B services to overseas customers are outside the scope of UK VAT, and the customer accounts for tax under the reverse charge. Most B2C services are taxable where the supplier belongs, so UK VAT applies to a UK supplier's consumer customer abroad.

Digital services

Digital services have their own place-of-supply rules, generally based on the customer's location. A UK business selling digital services to consumers in another country may need to register there, and a non-UK business selling to UK consumers may need to register for UK VAT.

Questions

Do I charge VAT on exports?
No. Exports of goods are zero-rated when you keep evidence that the goods left the UK. Zero-rated is still a taxable supply, so it goes on your VAT return.
Do I charge VAT to overseas business clients?
Usually no for B2B services, because the place of supply is the customer's country. The customer accounts for VAT under the reverse charge.
What is postponed VAT accounting?
A way for VAT-registered importers to declare import VAT on their VAT return instead of paying it at the border. It has been available since 1 January 2021.
Is the £135 import threshold a VAT exemption?
No. It is a value threshold that changes the import rules for low-value consignments. It is not a general exemption from VAT.
Does Northern Ireland use different VAT rates?
No. The rates are the same as the rest of the UK. The differences are in the rules for moving goods under the Windsor Framework.
Can I reclaim VAT on imports?
Yes, if you are VAT-registered, the goods are for business use, and you hold the import evidence. With postponed VAT accounting, the import VAT and the recovery appear on the same return.
