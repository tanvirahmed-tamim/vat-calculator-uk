VAT by business type

Maintained by the VAT in UK team · Last reviewed 10 November 2026 · 9 min read

VAT is one tax, but the practical rules change with what you sell and how the business is structured. This collection groups the guides by business type so you can go straight to the rules that apply to you.

In short

Most VAT-registered businesses charge 20% on taxable sales, but the edge cases differ by sector. Landlords deal with exempt rent and option to tax, builders with the domestic reverse charge, online sellers with marketplaces, and charities with business versus non-business income. Find your type below.

Why business type matters

Two businesses with the same turnover can have very different VAT obligations. A consultant selling services to overseas companies may charge no UK VAT, while a shop selling the same value of goods charges 20% on everything. A landlord's rent may be exempt, and a builder's work may fall under the domestic reverse charge.

The arithmetic stays simple: net × (1 + rate) for a sale, gross ÷ (1 + rate) for a purchase. The judgement is about liability (which rate applies) and place of supply (whether the sale is in the UK at all).

What every business still has to do

  • Register once taxable turnover passes £90,000 in a rolling 12 months, or is expected to pass it in the next 30 days.
  • Keep digital records and file through Making Tax Digital software.
  • Charge the right rate at the tax point and show it on a valid VAT invoice.
  • Reclaim input tax only where the cost is for business use and supported by evidence.
  • Keep records for six years.

When to take advice

Sector rules carry penalties for getting them wrong, and some are genuinely complex: partial exemption, option to tax, the margin scheme, TOMS, and cross-border place of supply. If a supply is large, mixed, or borderline, ask HMRC for a ruling or take professional advice before you invoice.

Questions

Do freelancers need to register for VAT?
Only when taxable turnover exceeds £90,000 in a rolling 12 months, or is expected to exceed it in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration is allowed earlier, which can help if you buy a lot from VAT-registered suppliers.
Do landlords charge VAT on rent?
No on residential rent, which is exempt. Commercial rent is also exempt unless the landlord opts to tax the property, after which 20% VAT applies.
Do online sellers charge VAT on every sale?
Only if the seller is VAT-registered and the sale is taxable. Sales through a UK marketplace can be handled by the platform for overseas sellers, so check who is responsible before you invoice.
Do builders charge VAT on new builds?
No. The first sale of a new build is zero-rated when the conditions are met. General repairs and maintenance are standard-rated at 20%, and some renovations use 5%.
Can charities reclaim VAT?
Only on business activities, not on non-business income such as grants and donations. Some charity reliefs allow zero-rating on purchases, so check the specific relief before assuming a cost is VAT-free.
Do consultants charge VAT to overseas clients?
Usually no for B2B services, because the place of supply is the customer's country. Keep evidence that the customer is in business and where they belong.
