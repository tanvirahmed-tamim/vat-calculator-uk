Reference

UK VAT glossary

Last reviewed 19 November 2026

The terms that come up on VAT invoices, returns and HMRC guidance, explained without jargon.

A B C D E F G I L M N O P R S T V W X Z

A

Acquisition VAT
VAT accounted for on goods received in Northern Ireland from an EU member state. It is declared in box 2 and normally recovered in box 4 of the UK VAT return.

Related guide VAT calculator

Annual accounting scheme
A scheme that lets eligible businesses make instalment payments during the year and file one annual VAT return, instead of quarterly returns.

Related guide VAT calculator

Assessment
A formal demand from HMRC for VAT that it considers unpaid, usually issued after a compliance check or a late return.

Related guide VAT calculator

B

B2B
Business-to-business. For most services, a B2B supply is taxed where the customer belongs, which is why a UK business often charges no UK VAT to an overseas business customer.

Related guide VAT calculator

B2C
Business-to-consumer. For most services, a B2C supply is taxed where the supplier belongs, so a UK business usually charges UK VAT to a consumer abroad.

Related guide VAT calculator

Bad debt relief
Relief that lets a supplier reclaim output VAT on an unpaid invoice after a set period, provided the debt has been written off and the conditions are met.

Related guide VAT calculator

Blocked input tax
VAT that cannot be reclaimed even though the business is VAT-registered. Common examples include business entertainment of UK clients and most cars available for private use.

Related guide VAT on expenses calculator

Business activity
An activity carried on in the course of business for VAT purposes. Grants and donations with nothing supplied in return are usually outside the scope, which affects what a charity can reclaim.

Related guide VAT calculator

Business entertainment
Hospitality provided to business contacts, such as meals, drinks and events. VAT on entertaining UK clients is blocked, while staff entertainment and some overseas customer entertainment can be recovered.

Related guide VAT on expenses calculator

Business rates
A local property tax on non-domestic property, collected by councils or, in Northern Ireland, by Land & Property Services. It is not VAT.

Related guide VAT calculator

C

Catering
Food and drink supplied ready for consumption, usually standard-rated at 20%. It includes restaurant meals, event catering and hot takeaway food.

Related guide VAT calculator

CIS
The Construction Industry Scheme, which governs tax deductions on payments to subcontractors. It interacts with the domestic reverse charge for VAT on construction services.

Related guide VAT calculator

Climate Change Levy
A separate environmental tax on business energy use. It appears on energy bills alongside VAT and is not itself a VAT charge.

Related guide VAT calculator

Composite supply
A single supply made up of several elements that are so closely linked that they form one indivisible supply, taxed at the rate of the main element.

Related guide VAT calculator

Consideration
Everything received in return for a supply, including money, goods, services and sometimes non-monetary benefits. It is the amount on which VAT is calculated.

Related guide VAT calculator

Credit note
A document that reduces the amount previously invoiced, for example after a return or a discount. It adjusts output tax in the period it is issued.

Related guide VAT calculator

D

De minimis
The limit below which exempt input tax can be treated as taxable and recovered in full. For partial exemption, exempt input tax must be no more than £625 a month on average and no more than half of total input tax.

Related guide VAT calculator

Deliberate error
An error made knowingly, or recklessly as to whether it was correct. It must always be reported separately and can attract a penalty of up to 70%, or up to 100% if concealed.

Related guide VAT calculator

Deregistration threshold
The turnover level at which a registered business can apply to cancel its VAT registration. It is currently £88,000, tested against expected taxable turnover for the next 12 months, not the last 12.

Related guide Registration threshold tool

Devolved tax
A tax set by a devolved administration rather than the UK government, such as LBTT in Scotland or LTT in Wales. VAT is not devolved.

Related guide VAT calculator

Disbursement
A cost paid on behalf of a client and recharged at exactly the same amount. Genuine disbursements are outside the scope of VAT, but strict conditions apply.

Related guide VAT calculator

Distance selling
Sales of goods to customers in another country without a local establishment. Cross-border distance sales can require a VAT registration in the customer's country.

Related guide VAT calculator

Domestic reverse charge
A rule that moves the duty to account for VAT from the supplier to the customer for certain supplies, notably specified construction and CIS services.

Related guide Reverse charge calculator

E

Error correction
The process of fixing a mistake on a VAT return, either on the next return or by notifying HMRC separately when the error is above the limits.

Related guide VAT calculator

Exempt supply
A supply that is within the scope of VAT but charged no VAT, such as many financial, insurance and education services. Input VAT on costs used to make exempt supplies is generally blocked.

Related guide VAT calculator

Export
Goods or services supplied to a customer outside the UK. Exports of goods are usually zero-rated with evidence, while many B2B services are outside the scope of UK VAT.

Related guide VAT calculator

F

Flat Rate Scheme
A simplified scheme where eligible businesses pay VAT at a fixed percentage of VAT-inclusive turnover rather than accounting for VAT on each sale and purchase.

Related guide Flat Rate Scheme estimate

Fuel scale charge
A fixed amount added to output VAT when a business reclaims VAT on fuel used for private motoring in a company vehicle.

Related guide VAT calculator

G

Gross amount
The total price including VAT. Removing VAT from a gross amount means dividing by 1 plus the rate, not subtracting the rate.

Related guide VAT calculator

Group registration
A way for companies under common control to register as a single VAT group, with one return covering the group's supplies.

Related guide VAT calculator

I

Import VAT
VAT charged on goods imported into the UK. A VAT-registered importer can usually recover it, and postponed VAT accounting lets it be declared on the VAT return.

Related guide VAT calculator

Input tax
VAT paid on business purchases and expenses. VAT-registered businesses usually reclaim it against output tax, subject to the blocked items and partial exemption rules.

Related guide VAT on expenses calculator

Invoice
A document that records a sale, the parties, the goods or services, the net amount, the VAT rate and the VAT charged. A valid VAT invoice is normally required to reclaim input tax.

Related guide VAT calculator

L

Land and Buildings Transaction Tax
The property transaction tax in Scotland, commonly shortened to LBTT. It replaced SDLT and is separate from VAT.

Related guide VAT calculator

Land Transaction Tax
The property transaction tax in Wales, commonly shortened to LTT. It replaced SDLT and is separate from VAT.

Related guide VAT calculator

Late payment penalty
A penalty for paying VAT after the due date. The first penalty is 3% at day 15 and a further 3% at day 30, then a second penalty at 10% a year from day 31.

Related guide VAT calculator

Limited cost trader
A business whose spending on relevant goods is a small proportion of turnover. Limited cost traders must use the 16.5% Flat Rate Scheme percentage regardless of sector.

Related guide Flat Rate Scheme estimate

M

Making Tax Digital
HMRC's requirement for VAT-registered businesses to keep digital records and file VAT returns using compatible software.

Related guide VAT calculator

Margin scheme
A VAT accounting method for eligible second-hand goods, antiques, collectibles and used vehicles, where VAT is calculated on the margin rather than the full selling price.

Related guide Margin scheme calculator

Mixed supply
A supply containing separate elements that are distinct enough to be taxed at different VAT rates, unlike a composite supply.

Related guide VAT calculator

N

Net amount
The price before VAT is added, or the amount left after VAT is removed from a gross price.

Related guide VAT calculator

O

Option to tax
An election that makes a commercial property taxable at 20%, allowing the landlord to recover VAT on qualifying costs. It usually lasts 20 years and must be notified to HMRC.

Related guide VAT calculator

OSS
The One Stop Shop, an EU scheme that lets a business account for VAT on EU B2C sales through one EU member state instead of registering in each country.

Related guide VAT calculator

Out of scope
A transaction that is not a supply for VAT purposes at all, for example some transfers of a business as a going concern or certain private sales.

Related guide VAT calculator

Output tax
VAT charged on a business's sales. It is recorded on the VAT return and offset against input tax.

Related guide VAT calculator

P

Partial exemption
The set of rules that limits how much input VAT can be reclaimed when a business makes both taxable and exempt supplies.

Related guide VAT calculator

Place of supply
The rules that decide which country's VAT applies to a supply, particularly important for cross-border services and goods.

Related guide VAT calculator

Points-based penalty
The late filing penalty regime for VAT returns. Each late return earns a point, and a £200 penalty applies once the threshold for the filing frequency is reached.

Related guide VAT calculator

Postponed VAT accounting
A method that lets VAT-registered importers declare and reclaim import VAT on the same VAT return instead of paying it at the border.

Related guide VAT calculator

R

Reasonable excuse
An unexpected event outside your control that explains a failure to meet a VAT obligation. It can reduce or cancel a penalty, but it is interpreted narrowly.

Related guide VAT calculator

Record keeping
The legal requirement to keep VAT invoices, the VAT account and supporting records, normally for six years.

Related guide VAT calculator

Reduced rate
The 5% VAT rate. It applies to a specific list of supplies, including domestic gas and other domestic fuels, children's car seats and some home renovations. Qualifying domestic electricity in Great Britain is temporarily 0% from 1 October 2026 to 30 June 2027.

Related guide VAT calculator

Registration threshold
The level of taxable turnover at which VAT registration becomes compulsory. It is currently £90,000 over a rolling 12-month period.

Related guide Registration threshold tool

Representative member
The member of a VAT group that files the group's VAT return and deals with HMRC on behalf of the other members.

Related guide VAT calculator

Residual input tax
VAT on costs used for both taxable and exempt supplies, or for the business as a whole. It is apportioned under the standard method or an approved special method.

Related guide VAT calculator

Reverse charge
A mechanism where the customer, rather than the supplier, accounts for VAT. It applies to certain cross-border services and to the domestic reverse charge for construction.

Related guide Reverse charge calculator

Rounding
Adjusting a VAT figure to the nearest penny. Normal rounding is expected, and the method should be applied consistently across invoices.

Related guide VAT calculator

S

SDLT
Stamp Duty Land Tax, the property transaction tax in England and Northern Ireland. It is separate from VAT.

Related guide VAT calculator

Simplified invoice
A shorter invoice that retailers can issue for retail supplies under £250 including VAT, showing less detail than a full VAT invoice.

Related guide VAT calculator

Standard method
The default partial exemption method, which apportions residual input tax using the value of taxable supplies divided by total supplies.

Related guide VAT calculator

Standard rate
The 20% VAT rate that applies to most goods and services. It has been at 20% since 4 January 2011.

Related guide VAT calculator

Supply
A sale, lease, gift or other provision of goods or services in the course of business. VAT is charged on taxable supplies.

Related guide VAT calculator

T

Tax point
The date that determines the VAT period and rate for a supply, usually the earlier of the invoice date and the date payment is received.

Related guide VAT calculator

Taxable person
A person or business that is, or is required to be, registered for VAT and makes taxable supplies.

Related guide VAT calculator

Taxable supply
A supply of goods or services that is standard-rated, reduced-rate or zero-rated. It excludes exempt supplies and supplies outside the scope of VAT.

Related guide VAT calculator

TOGC
Transfer of a business as a going concern. The sale is treated as neither a supply of goods nor a supply of services, so no VAT is charged, provided the conditions are met.

Related guide VAT calculator

TOMS
The Tour Operators Margin Scheme. It lets a tour operator account for VAT on the margin on certain travel packages rather than on the full selling price.

Related guide VAT calculator

V

VAT account
The summary of output tax, input tax and net VAT for each return period, kept as part of the business's VAT records.

Related guide VAT calculator

VAT error notification
The online route for telling HMRC about a VAT error that is above the method 1 limits, or about any deliberate error. A letter containing the same details is the fallback if the online service cannot be used.

Related guide VAT calculator

VAT fraction
The fraction used to extract VAT from a gross amount. At 20% it is 1/6; at 5% it is 1/21.

Related guide VAT fraction calculator

VAT inspection
A check by HMRC of a business's VAT records, and sometimes its premises, to confirm that returns are accurate and supported.

Related guide VAT calculator

VAT invoice
A valid invoice that meets HMRC's requirements and lets a VAT-registered customer reclaim input tax.

Related guide VAT calculator

VAT return
The periodic declaration of output tax, input tax and the net amount payable to or reclaimable from HMRC.

Related guide VAT calculator

W

Windsor Framework
The post-Brexit arrangement governing goods movements involving Northern Ireland, including special VAT rules and XI VAT numbers.

Related guide VAT calculator

X

XI VAT number
A VAT number beginning with XI that identifies a Northern Ireland business for certain goods movements under the Windsor Framework.

Related guide VAT calculator

Z

Zero-rated supply
A taxable supply charged at 0%, such as most food, books and children's clothing. It still belongs on the VAT return and usually allows input VAT recovery.

Related guide VAT calculator
